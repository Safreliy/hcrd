# AIOps C3: independent real KPI transfer

The development-selected HCRD-area-density to spectral-residual hybrid did not
transfer.  On the 13 fixed primary phase-2 KPIs, mean AUC-PR was 0.367469
versus 0.385041 for raw spectral residual.  Difference -0.017572, bootstrap
95% interval [-0.118608, 0.113572], t p=0.7825, Wilcoxon p=0.3757, exact
sign-flip p=0.8040, and 3/0/10 wins/ties/losses.

Across all 29 phase-2 KPIs, the difference was -0.011152 with interval
[-0.059840, 0.049060] and 9/0/20 wins/ties/losses.  Direct HCRD was examined
only secondarily: on the primary set it exceeded raw spectral residual by
0.030656, but the interval [-0.146851, 0.163362] was inconclusive; it differed
from raw absolute deviation by -0.003075 with interval
[-0.158331, 0.119894].  No real-KPI superiority claim is supported.

The negative transfer is scientifically useful: temporal analysis of polygon
area is possible, but spectral residual on that derived series is not a
generically improving hybrid.
