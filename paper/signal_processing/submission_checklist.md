# Signal Processing submission checklist

Prepared against the current Elsevier journal page and publisher-wide author
instructions on 2026-08-26.

- Article type: Original Research Article.
- Scope: novel signal-processing tools, statistical signal processing,
  biomedical signal processing, software, and new applications.
- Length: no more than 30 single-column, double-spaced pages including figures,
  tables, and references.
- Manuscript: `hcrd_signal_processing.pdf`, 30 A4 pages, built with
  `elsarticle` in review layout with line numbers and numerical references.
- Supplement: upload `../hcrd_supplement.pdf` as a separate supplementary file.
- Highlights: upload `highlights.txt` separately; five bullets, each at most 85
  characters.
- Optional graphical abstract: if included, upload `graphical_abstract.pdf` or
  the 300 dpi PNG as a separate file. The artwork is 2.5:1, exceeds 1328 by 531
  pixels, and is generated from the released HCRD implementation.
- Keywords: six are included after the abstract.
- Required statements: code/data availability, CRediT contribution, funding,
  competing interest, and generative-AI disclosure are included before the
  references.
- Public implementation: https://github.com/Safreliy/hcrd is cited in the
  manuscript.
- LaTeX source: `hcrd_signal_processing_source.zip` is flat and can be rebuilt
  with `python build_submission_source.py`.

Before upload, add the preferred corresponding-author email in the Elsevier
submission form and front matter, verify whether the editor requests an ORCID,
replace the GitHub-only release with a DOI-backed archival release if available,
and confirm that all files are named exactly as selected in Editorial Manager.
