Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript "Hierarchical Convexity-Run Decomposition:
Finite-Sample Chord-Lobe Recovery and Interpretable LC-MS Transfer" for
publication as an Original Research Article in *Signal Processing*.

The manuscript introduces a finite nonlinear signal transform that partitions
a sampled graph by discrete-curvature sign, replaces each run by its endpoint
chord, and recurses on the retained knots. The resulting signed residuals form
an exact, interpretable hierarchy. The paper establishes finite termination,
linear total sparse work, recovery guarantees under noise and approximate
sampled joins, a sharp discontinuity boundary, and stable companion
representations.

The principal application is cross-study quality curation of pre-bounded
liquid-chromatography mass-spectrometry waveforms. In both pre-specified
no-refit transfer directions, the multilevel representation improved average precision over an
independently reimplemented quality score. The conditional result remains
positive under target retention-time block resampling. Equal-dimensional,
implementation, source-refit, file-deletion, and external-laboratory
sensitivities define both the practical value and the limits of the method.

The work fits the journal's scope in novel and statistical signal-processing
tools, interpretable signal representations, open-source software, and new
analytical measurement applications. The implementation, protocols, compact
results, and reproducibility instructions are publicly available at
https://github.com/Safreliy/hcrd.

This manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has
been approved by its sole author. The author declares no competing interests
and no specific external funding.
